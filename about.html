About PC Games vs PlayStation

We are an independent editorial site covering PlayStation news and guides, and comparing PC gaming against it honestly. No console manufacturer or GPU vendor pays us for anything we publish.

What this site is for

The name of this site is the question we keep getting asked. Should you buy a PlayStation or build or buy a gaming PC? Both answers are reasonable, and the right one depends on your games, your television, your room, your budget and how much tinkering you enjoy.

We cover PlayStation news, settings and guides because that is where most of our readers are. When we put something side by side with PC, we do it because the comparison is genuinely useful, not because one side pays us.

Our PS5 or PC buying guide is the place to start if that is the decision you are making.

How we test and compare

Comparisons here are based on published specifications, our own testing where we have run the test ourselves, and clearly attributed reporting from the manufacturers and developers involved. Where we are repeating a figure that came from a hardware vendor, we say so rather than presenting it as our own measurement.

  • Every price is dated. Hardware prices move constantly. When we state a price we state the date we last checked it and the region it applies to.
  • Generalisations are labelled. Claims about a whole platform, such as how PC ports perform in general, are described as generalisations rather than measurements of one game.
  • We say which side we are on. If we own a PlayStation or a gaming PC, we say so on the page. Equipment ownership is disclosed so you can judge for yourself.
  • No paid verdicts. We do not accept payment to change a conclusion. If we ever run advertising, it will be clearly marked and will never be attached to a comparison conclusion.

Corrections policy

Games, hardware and firmware all change. If we get something wrong we want to fix it, and we want to know. Send us the page, what is inaccurate, and the correct information, ideally with a source.

We correct silently for small fixes such as a wrong price or a typo. Where a correction changes a conclusion or a recommendation, we update the page and note the change with a date.
