FAQ

Frequently asked questions

About this site, how we compare PlayStation and PC, and how the bits of interactivity here work.

About this site

Is PC Games vs PlayStation affiliated with Sony?

No. This is an independent editorial site. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or connected to Sony Interactive Entertainment or any other console or hardware company. Platform names are used only to describe what we write about.

Who writes this site?

PC Games vs PlayStation is operated as an independent site. Editorial standards, how we compare hardware and how we handle corrections are described on the About page.

Are you paid to recommend a particular platform?

No. No manufacturer, platform holder, publisher or retailer has ever paid us to change a conclusion, and we do not run paid placement inside our comparisons. If a page contains advertising in future, it will be labelled as such and kept away from our conclusions.

How old is the information?

Every guide shows the date it was last updated, and every hardware price shows the date and region it was checked for. Console firmware and graphics card pricing change often, so treat older figures as a snapshot rather than a current quote. If you spot something out of date, please tell us.

Do you cover PlayStation 4 and PC as well as PS5?

Yes. Most of our console coverage applies to the PS5, and we still cover PS4 where a topic matters. PC coverage focuses on gaming desktops and the graphics card decisions that matter at 1440p and 4K.

PS5 versus PC questions

So which is better, PS5 or PC?

Neither is universally better, and we are not going to pretend otherwise. A PlayStation wins on setup simplicity and on games you cannot play anywhere else. A PC wins on settings freedom, upgrade path and usually on cost per year over several years. The PS5 or PC buying guide walks through the decision properly.

Do you have a house favourite?

No, and if we ever did we would be ruining our own site. Our guides are written for readers with different budgets, rooms and tastes, so we explain trade-offs rather than naming a winner.

Are your comparison figures benchmarks you ran?

Where we state a specific measurement we have run the test ourselves and we say on the page what hardware we used. Where a figure comes from a manufacturer, a developer or a publication, we attribute it. Statements about a whole platform are labelled as generalisations rather than measurements of one game.

Are PC ports always better than the console version?

Often, not always. A port can take advantage of a much wider range of settings, but a console build tuned by its developers can hold a steady frame rate more reliably on fixed hardware. Our guide on PC ports versus console versions covers where each side gains ground.

The mini game and leaderboard

What is the mini game?

It is a small browser runner we built so this site has something playable. It runs entirely in your browser with no download, no account and no advertising. You can play it here.

Is the score system real?

Yes. You earn a point for every frame you survive and 100 points for each gold token you collect, and hitting an obstacle ends the run. It is a small but complete skill game, so your scores reflect how well you actually played.

How good is the game, really?

Honest answer: it is very small. It is a single-screen platformer with falling obstacles and collectable tokens, written from scratch in a few hundred lines. It is here to be played for a few minutes, not to compete with anything on either platform. It costs us nothing and needs no download.

Where is my score stored?

In your own browser, using local storage on this device. It is never uploaded to us and we cannot see it. Clearing your browser data for this site deletes it permanently. The leaderboard shows only your own scores from this browser.

Do you need an account?

No, and we do not offer one. We do not run sign-up, and we do not ask for personal details to read anything on this site.

Contact and privacy

How do I report a mistake?

Use the contact form. Tell us the page, what is wrong and what the accurate information is. Small corrections we make silently; anything that changes a conclusion gets a dated note on the page.

What happens to my contact form message?

The form has no server behind it. Submitting it opens your own email application with the message written out, so you review it and press send. The message goes from your email provider to us and this website never receives it. Email us directly at [contact address not set].

Not published yet? If no contact address has been published, the form will refuse to submit and tell you so, rather than quietly opening an email that goes nowhere. Check the contact page before relying on it.

Do you use cookies or track me?

We do not set advertising or analytics cookies and we do not run third-party trackers. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers; we do not control what that request exposes. Full detail is on our Privacy Policy.

There was a pop-up about age ratings when I loaded a page.

That is a one-time informational notice reminding you that we cover games across all age ratings. It appears once per browsing session, it never blocks content, no page is hidden behind it, and no button will send you to another website. It exists because some games we discuss carry mature ratings and we would rather you know before reading.
