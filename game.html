How to score well

You gain a point for every frame you stay alive and 100 points for each gold token. Obstacles fall from the top of the screen and are never in the same place twice, so there is no single safe lane to memorise. Hitting one ends the run immediately.

Two habits get you most of the way: move constantly so the character is never parked under a falling obstacle, and jump only when a token is within reach rather than jumping constantly.

Why there is a game on a news site

People spend a lot of time comparing consoles and settings without ever playing anything. A tiny thing you can pick up for thirty seconds serves that better than another table of specifications.

It is also self-contained: no third-party game engine, no tracking, no network calls. Everything runs in your browser using the canvas element, and the only data written is your score in your browser's local storage.

If you would rather read, start with the PS5 or PC buying guide.

How your score is handled

Scores and token counts are saved in this browser's local storage on this device. They are never uploaded to us, we cannot see them, and nobody else sees them. Clearing your browser data for this site deletes them for good.

That is also why the leaderboard shows your own runs on this device rather than a global ranking of other players. There is no server holding a list.
