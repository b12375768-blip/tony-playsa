PC Games vs PlayStation: which platform actually suits you?

We publish independent PlayStation news and guides, and we compare PC and PlayStation side by side on the things that matter: what it costs to own, how it performs, how it feels to play, and which games you can actually get.

Read the PS5 or PC guide Browse all guides

The short answer

Neither platform wins outright. Pick PlayStation if you want a console that works straight out of the box and value exclusives. Pick PC if you want the best settings, the widest library and cheaper upgrades over time.
