Privacy Policy

The short version: this site has no accounts, sets no advertising cookies, runs no third-party trackers, and sends nothing you type anywhere. Here is the detail.

Last updated: 12 January 2026. This policy applies to every page on this site.

1. Who runs this site

PC Games vs PlayStation is an independent editorial site. The contact address for any privacy question is [contact address not set].

Publisher details are incomplete. The name and contact address of whoever operates this site have not been confirmed yet, and this policy is not valid for publication until they are. Under several privacy regulations, including the GDPR, a controller must be identifiable. Add the operator's legal name and a working contact address before publishing.

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or connected to Sony Interactive Entertainment Inc. or any other platform, hardware or software company. Your use of a third-party platform or store is governed by that company's own privacy policy, not this one.

2. What we collect

Almost nothing, by design. There is no registration, no sign-up, no newsletter database and no user account anywhere on this site.

  • Local game scores. If you play the mini game, the result is written to your browser's local storage on your own device. It is never transmitted to us, we cannot see it, and we have no copy of it.
  • A session flag. The age-ratings notice stores one flag in your browser's session storage so it does not reappear on every page during the same visit. It is deleted when you close the tab and is never sent to us.
  • Email you send us. If you email us, we hold your message and address for as long as needed to reply and to keep a record of any correction you asked for. We do not add you to any list and we do not pass your details to anyone.

Nothing else is collected. In particular we do not collect your name, address, phone number, payment details, precise location, contacts or calendar, because we never ask for them.

3. The contact form

Our contact form has no server behind it. When you submit it, the page builds a message in your browser and hands it to your own email application. You review it and press send.

The consequence, stated plainly: this website never receives what you type into that form. The message travels directly from your email provider to our inbox, so our mail provider handles it under their privacy policy, not this one. There is no hidden endpoint logging submissions.

4. Cookies and advertising

We set no cookies. There are no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies, no affiliate cookies, no consent banner and no cross-site tracking on this site.

We run no third-party tracking scripts of any kind. No data is sold, rented or shared with data brokers.

5. Third-party services

Two external services are involved in serving a page:

  • Google Fonts. Our stylesheet loads typefaces from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. When your browser fetches those files, Google sees your IP address and user agent as any web host would. We do not control that. If you would rather avoid it, use a browser extension that blocks remote fonts, or read the site without loading them.
  • Outbound links. Some pages link to ESRB, PEGI and retailer or manufacturer sites. Those pages have their own privacy practices. Following such a link takes you to a site we do not control and are not responsible for.

We deliberately do not embed advertising networks, video players, social media widgets, maps or comment systems, precisely because each of those would hand third parties data about you.

6. Our legal bases

Where the law requires a lawful basis for processing personal data, we rely on legitimate interests for answering emails and keeping the site secure, and on consent where you have given it, such as a local score remaining on your device. Scores stay on your device until you clear them, which is treated as withdrawal of that consent.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access the personal data an organisation holds about you, to correct it, to have it erased, to restrict or object to its use, and to complain to a data protection authority.

Because we hold so little, these requests are usually quick. Email us and we will action a deletion or correction request well within any statutory deadline. We would rather do this on request than run a self-service portal we do not need.

8. Children

This site is written for a general audience and is not directed at children. Because we collect no personal data from anyone and run no accounts, there is no information about a child for us to hold, disclose or delete.

9. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. This is a static site with no user database, no login system and no checkout, which removes the usual categories of breach. No system is perfectly secure, and email is not encrypted end to end, so please do not send us anything highly sensitive.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of the page. If a change materially affects how we handle data, we will say so prominently on the homepage rather than relying on you to re-read this page.

[contact address not set]

This site has no registered postal address. Please do not send physical mail. Our Terms & Conditions cover the rest of the rules for using this site.
